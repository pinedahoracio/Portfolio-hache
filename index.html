I'm Horacio "hache" Pineda, a graphic designer with a passion for building brands that resonate. I combine visual excellence with strategic thinking — whether it's a logo that defines a company, an editorial layout that tells a story, or a UX flow that guides users effortlessly.

My work spans brand identity, editorial design, advertising, digital and out-of-home campaigns, UX/UI, and marketing. I thrive in collaborative environments, and I bring the same precision and care to a billboard as I do to a social media animation.

The person

Horacio
"hache" Pineda
Ottawa · Canada

Hache is my personal brand — and let me tell you, it's a wild ride! I'm located in Ottawa, Canada, but my heart beats to a Mexican rhythm, born and bred in the vibrant chaos of Mexico City. I'm all about delivering killer design solutions tailored to your unique needs. Think of me as your design luchador, ready to rumble with any creative challenge!

With a passion for creativity and innovation that burns hotter than a habanero, I approach each project with a fresh perspective — ensuring our designs don't just meet expectations; they smash them. I'm fluent in both English and Spanish, so we can talk tacos or tech, whatever floats your boat.

When I'm not wrestling with pixels, you'll find me throwing down at the CrossFit box, snapping shots with my camera, or getting lost in a good flick. Quality, originality, and clear communication are my superpowers. Let's make some design magic happen.
